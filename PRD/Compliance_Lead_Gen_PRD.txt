PRD: COMPLIANCE & CYBERSECURITY LEAD GENERATION ENGINE

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
The objective is to build a modular automated lead generation system that identifies US-based healthcare providers and vendors (HIPAA focus) and financial/tech firms (Cybersecurity focus). The system will scrape business data, enrich it with decision-maker contact info, analyze their current compliance posture, and inject them into a Woodpecker email campaign.

2. MODULE 1: SCRAPING LOGIC (THE DISCOVERY ENGINE)
Goal: Identify high-intent targets and extract baseline digital footprints.

A. Targeting Parameters:
- Search Sources: Google Maps API, NPI Registry, HealthGrades, niche directories.
- Queries: "Dental practices in [City]", "Outpatient Surgery Centers", "Managed IT Services [State]".
- Extraction: Company Name, Website URL, Physical Address, Phone Number.

B. Technical Requirements:
- Use Playwright or Selenium for JavaScript-heavy rendering.
- Filtering: Target SMBs (10-200 employees); exclude enterprise-level entities.
- Anti-Detection: Implement rotating user-agents, proxy rotation, and random sleep intervals (jitter).

3. MODULE 2: ENRICHMENT LOGIC (LEAD INTELLIGENCE)
Goal: Transform a URL into a contactable lead with personalized context.

A. Contact Discovery:
- Use Apollo.io.
- Personas: Practice Manager, IT Director, Compliance Officer, CEO/Founder.

B. Compliance Gap Analysis (The "Hook"):
- Scripted Scan: Check website for "Privacy Policy", "HIPAA Notice", or "Terms of Use".
- Tech Stack Detection: Use Wappalyzer/BuiltWith API to identify medical software (EHR/EMR).
- Security Audit: Check for SSL/HTTPS presence.

C. Scoring & Normalization:
- Lead Scoring: Rank leads based on industry relevance and detected gaps.
- Email Verification: Final validation via NeverBounce or Hunter to ensure <5% bounce rate.

4. MODULE 3: OUTREACH DISPATCH (WOODPECKER INTEGRATION)
Goal: Programmatically push leads into automated email sequences.

A. Technical Configuration:
- API: Woodpecker REST API (v2).
- Authentication: x-api-key header.
- Endpoint: POST https://api.woodpecker.co/rest/v1/add_prospects_campaign

B. Data Mapping:
- Email: [Verified Email]
- First Name: [Lead First Name]
- Company: [Company Name]
- Snippet 1 (The Hook): Specific compliance gap (e.g., "Missing HIPAA privacy link").
- Snippet 2: Industry niche for personalization.

C. Logic Flow:
- Deduplication: Check against existing prospect list.
- Batching: Push in batches of 50 to respect rate limits.
- Dry Run Mode: Log payload locally for validation before live injection.

5. SUCCESS METRICS
- Volume: 500+ verified leads per week.
- Quality: < 5% email bounce rate.
- Accuracy: 80% of leads categorized correctly by industry.

6. ETHICS & COMPLIANCE
- Adherence to CAN-SPAM Act.
- Compliance with platform Terms of Service (ToS) regarding scraping.
- Implementation of human-like browsing patterns.
